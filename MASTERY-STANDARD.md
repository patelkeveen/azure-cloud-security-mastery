# Mastery Standard

A topic is complete only when it is understandable, deployable, supportable, secure, and defensible in a customer conversation.

## Required artifacts per topic

- Concept explanation: fundamentals, intermediate behavior, and expert trade-offs.
- Architecture: diagram, dependencies, boundaries, identity flow, network flow, and data flow.
- Commands: portal, CLI, PowerShell, Graph, Bicep, Terraform, or API steps with permissions and rollback.
- Lab: setup, implementation, verification, cleanup, cost controls, and screenshots or sanitized logs when useful.
- Break/fix: at least one deliberate failure, diagnosis path, remediation, prevention, and RCA.
- Security: least privilege, secrets, logging, threat model, audit evidence, and compliance mapping.
- Operations: monitoring, alerting, SLO/SLA, backup/restore, runbook, escalation, and handover.
- Customer case: requirements, constraints, design decision, risk register, test plan, training, and acceptance criteria.

## Completion levels

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Indexed | Topic exists in the inventory. |
| Researched | Official and supplemental resources are captured. |
| Lab planned | Lab steps, prerequisites, risks, and cleanup are designed. |
| Lab verified | Lab was executed and evidence was retained. |
| Operated | Monitoring, failure, repair, and runbook evidence exist. |
| Customer ready | HLD, LLD, SOPs, support model, and acceptance criteria exist. |

## Expert questions

For every capability, be able to answer: why this service, why not the alternative, what can fail, who owns it, what it costs, how it is secured, how it is monitored, how it is migrated, how it is rolled back, and what evidence proves success.
