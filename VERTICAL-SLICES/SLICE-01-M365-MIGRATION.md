# Slice 1 — M365 Migration Factory

Scenario: migrate a synthetic Google Workspace and on-premises Exchange estate to Microsoft 365.

## Required layers

Business requirements, compliance mapping, current-state inventory, identity/domain design, mail/data dependencies, HLD, LLD, tool selection, pilot, coexistence, pre-stage, delta, cutover, rollback, reconciliation, hypercare, operations, and handover.

## Failure scenario

Migration initialization fails because OAuth scopes or delegation are incomplete. Capture the error, prove the root cause, correct it, rerun safely, reconcile results, and record the limitation of the test environment.

## Evidence

Assessment, dependency map, migration matrix, wave plan, cutover checklist, rollback SOP, validation report, and customer communication pack.
