# Slice 5 — Customer Architecture Pack

Scenario: deliver a regulated customer design combining M365, Azure, identity, NHI, migration, security, and operations.

## Required layers

Discovery, stakeholders, requirements, compliance, current state, target state, HLD, LLD, ADRs, risks, licensing/cost, test plan, cutover, rollback, SOPs, handover, training, and executive defense.

## Failure scenario

A review rejects the design because privilege escalation and backup-protection evidence is insufficient. Remediate the design, add evidence, update the threat model, and document the review response.

## Evidence

Complete architecture pack, traceability matrix, risk register, configuration checklist, customer status report, acceptance criteria, handover, and post-implementation review.
