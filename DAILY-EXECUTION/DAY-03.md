# Day 3 — Collaboration Governance

## Outcome

Design secure and supportable SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams, Groups, and Power Platform governance.

## Tasks

1. Inventory sites, hubs, owners, permissions, external sharing, guest users, storage, retention, and sensitive content.
2. Configure only safe test policies for SharePoint and OneDrive sharing, unmanaged devices, and access boundaries.
3. Map Teams, channels, private/shared channels, meetings, external access, guest lifecycle, and app permissions.
4. Design M365 Group naming, expiration, ownership, and creation controls.
5. Review Power Platform environments, connectors, DLP, service accounts, and CoE governance concepts.

## Failure exercises

- Inherited-permission break.
- Orphaned site or Group owner.
- Guest sharing policy conflict.
- Teams policy precedence issue.
- OneDrive sync or ownership issue.

## Deliverables

Collaboration governance matrix, site hierarchy map, permission model, external-sharing decision, lifecycle SOP, and sanitized assessment report.

## Teach-back

Explain the difference between a Team, Group, SharePoint site, OneDrive, security group, and M365 group; then explain how permissions and external sharing create risk.
