# Day 10 — Architecture, Customer Delivery, and Defense

## Outcome

Convert technical work into professional-services artifacts and defend the decisions.

## Tasks

1. Produce HLD and LLD with trust boundaries, identity flows, network/data flows, IP schemas, policy matrices, dependencies, and assumptions.
2. Write ADRs for PHS versus PTA/ADFS, native versus specialist migration tooling, and managed identity versus service principal.
3. Produce configuration checklist, implementation plan, test plan, cutover, rollback, operations manual, SOPs, escalation matrix, and handover.
4. Map security and compliance requirements to evidence without claiming certification or audit results not actually achieved.
5. Conduct executive and technical teach-backs; record questions, weak answers, and unresolved gaps.

## Deliverables

Complete customer architecture pack, three ADRs, handover manual, interview-defense sheet, sanitized evidence index, and final readiness report.

## Teach-back

Explain the design to a business executive, a new engineer, a customer administrator, and a CISO. Use trade-offs, failure modes, cost, reliability, and operational ownership—not service-name recitation.
