# 20 — Azure Platform

> ⭐ **What this is:** the control plane — how Azure decides who may do what, where resources live,
> and what stops someone deleting production. ⭐ **The one sentence that organises the whole domain:
> RBAC is a union, Policy is an intersection — and an Owner cannot violate Policy.**

**When to read it:** ⭐ **before Azure labs, and before any governance conversation.** Assumed by
SC-300's privileged-access material and by AZ-500/AZ-104 objectives.

**Prerequisites:** [`../00-foundations/`](../00-foundations/), [`../10-networking/`](../10-networking/).

---

## Why this domain exists

⭐ **Identity answers "who are you". This domain answers "and what may you do here".** They are
separate systems that meet at a role assignment, and most Azure security incidents are a failure of
the second, not the first.

⭐ **The trap this domain removes:** assuming an Owner can do anything. ⭐ **Azure Policy denies
before RBAC is consulted** — which is why Policy, not RBAC, is how you make a rule that survives an
administrator.
