# Stage Gates

## Stage 1 — INDEXED

The capability is named in the curriculum and completeness register. No competence claim is made.

## Stage 2 — RESEARCH

Official documentation, resource notes, terminology, architecture, dependencies, and known limitations are recorded.

## Stage 3 — LAB-PLANNED

Prerequisites, setup, commands, scripts, expected outputs, verification, cleanup, cost controls, and failure scenarios are authored.

## Stage 4 — LAB-VERIFIED

The lab was executed in a safe development environment. Logs, outputs, test data, and cleanup evidence are retained.

## Stage 5 — OPERATED

A failure was injected, detected, diagnosed from evidence, remediated, monitored, and documented with an RCA and runbook.

## Stage 6 — CUSTOMER-READY

The capability has HLD/LLD, ADR, risk register, test plan, change/cutover, rollback, SOP, customer defense, and handover evidence.

## Production boundary

`CUSTOMER-READY` is not `PRODUCTION-VERIFIED`. Production status requires genuine professional experience and must never be inferred from a personal lab or AI-generated code.
