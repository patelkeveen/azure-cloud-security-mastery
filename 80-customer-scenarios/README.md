## ⭐ The universal chain — the one thing to carry into every vertical

```
Regulation → Control objective → Technical requirement → Entra feature → EVIDENCE ARTIFACT
└─ customer owns these two ─┘   └────────── you own these three ──────────┘
```

⭐ **The evidence artifact is the deliverable, not the config.** ⭐ **Most vendors jump from
regulation straight to a product demo**; writing links ② and ③ down, and making the customer own ②,
is the move that stops you promising compliance you cannot verify.

⚠ **You do not interpret law.** In every topic here, the customer's compliance officer, DPO or
safeguarding lead owns the interpretation and you own the implementation.

## ⭐ Questions that reorder a project

⭐ **These work far beyond their own vertical. Steal all of them.**

- ⭐ *"When your auditor asks who approved a privileged elevation last March, what do you show them?"*
- ⭐ *"Walk me through a nurse starting a shift on a shared workstation — every tap."*
- ⭐ *"How long from termination to access revoked — **measured**, not intended?"*
- ⭐ *"How does your vendor connect when a line is down at 2 a.m.?"*
- ⭐ *"Who will look after this after we finish — a person, by name?"*
